SignalPath

An AI Customer Success copilot concept for detecting onboarding risk, investigating the broken workflow step and preparing a customer-approved recovery experiment.

Independent portfolio concept using fictional accounts and synthetic data. No hospital relationship, production deployment or measured customer outcome.

THE OPERATING PROBLEM

Account health can show that risk exists without explaining where the workflow is failing.

A Customer Success Manager needs to know which account deserves attention, which part of onboarding changed, and what should be tested before recommending a solution.

Product insight Connect the warning signal to a transparent evidence trail—not an unexplained AI answer.

PRODUCT WORKFLOW

Detect the risk. Investigate the cause. Demonstrate the next step.

01

Detect

Rank fictional accounts using transparent trend and severity signals.

02

Investigate

Resolve the account warning into a module-level anomaly and human testing plan.

03

Demonstrate

Prepare a recovery workflow with explicit customer approvals and measurement.

WORKFLOW TESTING

Analytics identifies where to look; testing explains what may be wrong.

01

Reproduce the journey

Run self-service, staff-assisted and pre-visit paths.

02

Inspect field behaviour

Check required, conditional and repeated insurance fields.

03

Test device states

Compare mobile, desktop, refresh and back-navigation behaviour.

PROPOSED RECOVERY

Turn the investigation into a reviewable customer conversation.

01Simplify insurance capture in the test workflow02Add clearer guidance for required information03Compare suitable global modules or screeners04Instrument starts, completions, errors and abandonment

Approval rule: No production change is made until the customer reviews and approves the proposal.

PROPOSED PILOT

Measure whether the revised workflow helps before claiming success.

Confirm the baseline, test the revised insurance step with a defined cohort, compare performance and adopt, iterate or revert based on pre-agreed rules.

PRIMARY METRICModule completion

The percentage of users who finish the Insurance Information module.

Guardrails

  • Error rate
  • Staff escalations
  • Time to complete
  • Customer review and approval

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Synthetic evidence, prototype output and proposed experiment remain distinct.

The project demonstrates analysis and product reasoning. It does not claim that SignalPath has predicted churn, improved a hospital workflow or produced measured business impact.
